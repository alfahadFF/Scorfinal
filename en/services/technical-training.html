Technical Training

Advanced knowledge... Professional skills... Performance that makes a difference

At SCOR, we believe advanced technology reaches its full potential when paired with technical expertise and operational skills. That is why technical training is central to our commitment to developing capabilities, enhancing performance and improving the operation and maintenance of equipment.

Technical training is a key pillar of SCOR’s strategy to provide integrated solutions that go beyond supplying equipment and machinery, encompassing the transfer of technical knowledge, the development of practical skills, and the empowerment of customers and partners to make the most of their equipment’s capabilities.

Through specialised training programmes, we work to equip operators, technicians, engineers and maintenance teams with engineering knowledge, industrial experience and practical applications, supporting a thorough understanding of technical systems, operating and maintenance methods, and effective fault diagnosis.

Comprehensive training programmes for different needs

SCOR’s specialised training framework is built around three main tracks:

1. Technical training for employees

Developing technical and engineering capabilities and strengthening knowledge of the latest equipment technologies and operating systems, helping to raise professional performance and improve the quality of technical services.

2. Equipment operation and maintenance training

Preparing equipment operators and maintenance teams in correct and safe operating methods, periodic inspection and preventive maintenance procedures, and the use of different systems. This helps reduce faults, improve operational efficiency and extend equipment service life.

3. Technical training for dealers and authorised service centres

Providing technical teams at dealers, partners and service centres with the specialist knowledge needed to inspect equipment, diagnose faults and perform maintenance and repairs in accordance with SCOR’s approved technical standards.

Practical training grounded in knowledge and application

Technical training at SCOR is not limited to theory; it focuses on practical application and a precise engineering understanding of equipment components and systems.

The programmes include studying and inspecting key components, including engines, drivetrains, axles, hydraulic pumps, control valves, electrical and electronic circuits, and control and operating systems.

Technical topics centre on three main disciplines:

  • Mechanical systems: engines, drivetrains, mechanical components, and inspection and maintenance techniques.
  • Hydraulic systems: pumps, valves, cylinders, control circuits, and methods for measuring performance and diagnosing faults.
  • Electrical and electronic systems: electrical circuits, sensors, control units, diagnostic systems, and fault detection and troubleshooting techniques.

Continuous development for future-ready capabilities

In line with our vision of continuous development, we work to keep pace with technical advances in the equipment and machinery sector and update training content to reflect modern technologies and actual operating requirements.

We also place special importance on sharing expertise with the technical teams of our customers, partners and dealers, strengthening their ability to address operational challenges efficiently and independently and helping to improve equipment readiness and production continuity.

At SCOR, we do more than supply equipment and technology: we build the expertise needed to operate, maintain and improve their performance. Real investment is not limited to machines; it also includes the minds and skills that guide them towards success.
