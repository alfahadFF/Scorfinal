Production Technology

Precision in manufacturing... Excellence in performance... Quality in every detail

At SCOR, we believe exceptional equipment begins with meticulous production processes that combine precision engineering, advanced industrial technologies and rigorous quality standards. We therefore work continuously to develop our production framework in step with industrial progress and provide equipment distinguished by strength, reliability and operational efficiency.

SCOR’s production philosophy integrates engineering design, material selection, manufacturing techniques, and assembly and testing processes to achieve products capable of meeting the demands of the most challenging operating conditions.

Through technical cooperation with factories and manufacturing partners, we work to apply considered production specifications and monitor the conformity of components and systems with the engineering requirements and quality standards defined for each product.

Precision engineering starts with material selection

Equipment quality begins with selecting materials and components suited to the intended use and expected operating loads.

We therefore pay particular attention to studying the mechanical properties of materials, their resistance to stress and wear, and their ability to withstand different operating conditions, balancing durability, weight, efficiency and service life.

We also focus on the conformity of materials and components with approved engineering drawings and technical specifications to ensure their integration within the equipment’s overall structure.

Advanced manufacturing and high-precision processing

The production framework includes manufacturing, forming, cutting, welding, machining and finishing processes, according to the nature of the components and the engineering requirements of each machine.

During these processes, attention is given to dimensional accuracy, surface quality, joint integrity and compatibility between parts, helping to improve assembly quality and reduce the risk of wear and faults caused by manufacturing inaccuracies.

Components undergo appropriate inspection and verification before moving to assembly, to check their conformity with technical specifications and design requirements.

Integrated assembly for high reliability

Assembly is a critical stage in ensuring equipment’s final performance, bringing mechanical, hydraulic and electrical systems together in a single operating system.

At this stage, we focus on precise component installation, setting connections and systems, and checking compatibility between moving and fixed parts to support stable response and balanced performance.

Drivetrains, engines, hydraulic components, control circuits and safety systems also receive particular attention to ensure their integration with operating requirements.

Performance tests that simulate real working conditions

Production does not end when assembly is complete; it extends to verifying the functional and operating performance of equipment.

The quality evaluation approach includes tests and inspections appropriate to each product, intended to assess system efficiency and response under different operating conditions.

Evaluation areas include:

  • Operational performance tests: checking equipment response and system efficiency while carrying out different tasks.
  • Load and durability tests: evaluating the ability of components and structures to withstand loads and stresses within design limits.
  • Hydraulic system tests: checking pressure, flow and response, and verifying connection integrity.
  • Movement and stability tests: evaluating drive, steering, braking and stability performance during operation.
  • Wear and endurance tests: studying the behaviour of components exposed to friction and stress under different conditions of use.
  • Attachment and equipment tests: checking the compatibility of optional attachments with the machine’s operating systems.

Manufacturing flexibility for project requirements

SCOR takes a flexible approach to supplying equipment in line with customer requirements and operating applications, through manufacturing and equipment options available in cooperation with our industrial partners.

This flexibility allows engine specifications, operating capacities, hydraulic systems, dimensions, attachments and additional equipment to be studied and defined so the equipment suits the required tasks.

We ensure customisation options undergo the necessary technical review to check their compatibility with the overall design and safety and performance requirements.

A quality commitment that extends beyond production

At SCOR, we see quality not as a final manufacturing stage, but as a fundamental principle accompanying the product from the first idea to its arrival on the work site.

Through continuous development, attention to engineering detail and cooperation with manufacturing partners, we strive to provide equipment that combines strength, precision and reliability and delivers real, long-term value to our customers.

SCOR... Where engineering precision becomes productive power and quality is the foundation of every achievement.
