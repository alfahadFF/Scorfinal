Research and Development

Innovating today... To shape the future of equipment and technology

At SCOR, we do not merely keep pace with industrial progress; we aspire to play an active role in shaping it. Research and development lie at the heart of our vision to offer more efficient equipment, more advanced technologies and engineering solutions that raise standards of performance, reliability and productivity.

We believe real innovation begins with understanding the challenges our customers face on site and turning them into opportunities to develop practical solutions that combine powerful performance, engineering precision and operating efficiency.

Research and development therefore form a strategic pillar of SCOR’s industrial philosophy. We focus on studying modern technologies, improving engineering designs, developing operating systems and enhancing the ability of equipment to work efficiently in different environments and conditions.

Advanced engineering driving innovation

Our research and development vision follows an integrated engineering approach that combines technical analysis, industrial design, performance studies and evaluation of component and system efficiency.

Core research and development areas include:

  • Mechanical engineering: developing structures and mechanical components, and improving durability and their ability to withstand loads and demanding operating conditions.
  • Hydraulic systems: studying the efficiency of hydraulic circuits, pumps and control valves, and improving system response, movement precision and energy efficiency.
  • Stress and durability analysis: evaluating the loads and forces acting on components and structures to enhance structural safety, reliability and service life.
  • Vibration and noise: studying sources of vibration and noise and working to improve operator comfort and equipment stability during operation.
  • Electrical and electronic systems: developing control, monitoring and diagnostic solutions, and studying the integration of smart technologies to improve operating efficiency.
  • Industrial design and operator environment: improving equipment functional design, access to controls, all-round visibility, and operator comfort and safety.

From idea to engineering solution

At SCOR, we see research and development as an integrated process that begins with the study of operating needs and moves through design, engineering analysis and evaluation of technical solutions, leading to the development of products and equipment that meet actual-use requirements.

We pay special attention to improving manufacturability, simplifying production and assembly processes, and developing components and equipment to balance quality, performance and operating cost.

We also focus on improving energy efficiency, reducing maintenance requirements, making technical components easier to access and enhancing equipment reliability under different working conditions.

Flexible engineering solutions for customer needs

We understand that project requirements vary according to the nature of the work, the operating environment and technical conditions. We therefore take a flexible approach to developing engineering solutions and tailoring equipment specifications to customers’ actual needs.

This flexibility includes studying options for engines, hydraulic systems, operating equipment, attachments, dimensions and performance capabilities, allowing solutions to be tailored to different applications.

This approach reflects SCOR’s commitment to developing products that go beyond conventional specifications, respond to real-world work requirements and deliver long-term operational value.

Continuous improvement... Quality begins with design

We believe equipment quality does not begin when manufacturing is complete; it takes shape from the earliest stages of design and development.

We therefore place improvements in quality, reliability and maintainability at the centre of our engineering approach, focusing on studying performance, analysing areas for improvement and developing solutions that help raise productivity and reduce operational downtime.

We also seek to use technical feedback and real-world operating experience to guide continuous improvement, helping SCOR equipment keep pace with evolving market and industrial application requirements.

Innovation is our responsibility to the future

For SCOR, research and development are not an isolated technical activity, but an integrated work culture built on engineering thinking, continuous improvement and the exploration of new possibilities.

Through this vision, we aspire to develop smarter, more efficient and more sustainable solutions that help advance the equipment and machinery industry and give our customers greater confidence in meeting work challenges.

At SCOR, we look not only for better ways to manufacture equipment, but also for new possibilities that redefine what that equipment can accomplish.
